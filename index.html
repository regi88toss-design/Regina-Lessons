️ Emphatic Storytelling

So/Such...that

Making Your Travel Stories More Dramatic!

️ Lesson Duration: 60 minutes

Let's Think! (5 min)

Breathtaking View

Discuss:
  • What's your most extreme travel/sport experience?
  • Think about hiking, cycling, or skiing!

⚠️ Camping Disasters

  • The storm was so violent that our tent collapsed
  • There were so many mosquitoes that we couldn't sleep
  • It was such heavy rain that everything got soaked
  • The fire was so wet that we couldn't cook
  • We were so tired that we camped on an ant hill

📖 Travel Blogger's Story (7 min)

My Moscow Marathon Adventure

Last weekend I ran my first marathon in Moscow. There were so many runners that the streets were packed! I was so nervous that I could barely sleep.

The weather was such a challenge that many people dropped out. It was so hot that we needed water every 2 km. There was so much support from locals that I felt like a celebrity!

After the race, my legs were so sore that I couldn't walk for three days. But it was such an amazing experience that I'm planning my next marathon!

Task: Find all so/such...that constructions. What types do you see?

❓ Check Understanding (6 min)

Concept Checking Questions:

  1. "so many runners" - are runners countable or uncountable?
  2. "so much support" - is support countable or uncountable?
  3. In "such a challenge" - why do we use "a"?
  4. Can we say "so much people"? Why/why not?
  5. Does the part BEFORE that describe cause or result?

📐 Grammar Formula (8 min)

SO + Adjective/Adverb + THAT
SO MUCH + Uncountable Noun + THAT
SO MANY + Plural Noun + THAT
SUCH (a/an) + Adjective + Noun + THAT

SO:

  • so steep that...
  • so fast that...
  • so tired that...

SO MUCH/MANY:

  • so much traffic that...
  • so many people that...

SUCH:

  • such a challenge that...
  • such experienced guides that...
  • such terrible weather that...

✏️ Practice - Click to Reveal! (10 min)

Pronunciation Drill (5 min)

Focus: Stress on adjective/noun BEFORE "that", falling tone AFTER

Pattern 1: SO + adjective

"It was so AMAZing ↗ that I couldn't beLIEVE it ↘"

"The trail was so STEEP ↗ that my legs were SHAKing ↘"

Pattern 2: SUCH + noun

"It was such a CHALLenge ↗ that we STOPPED "

"There were so MANY people ↗ that we LEFT "
